# DEMO FLOW

Target length: approximately 4–5 minutes.

## Step 1 — Beneficiary voice

Use a scripted/demo voice statement that is clearly synthetic/demo data.

Example:

> "Main tractor aur pump repair karta hoon, papa ke saath kaam karta hoon. Chhoti-moti machine repair kar leta hoon, lekin certificate nahi hai."

The interface labels the resulting skill evidence as self-reported.

## Step 2 — Structured skills

Show:
- machinery repair
- agricultural equipment repair
- basic troubleshooting

Only display skills actually represented in the canonical demo extraction.

## Step 3 — Clarification

Ask:
- employment vs self-employment
- mobility/access
- relevant experience details.

## Step 4 — Candidate pathways

Show up to 3 supported candidates.

Do not use invented percentages.

## Step 5 — Explain

Open one pathway:
- source-backed qualification
- supporting skills
- gaps
- access
- evidence status.

## Step 6 — Stress test

Reduce training capacity or mark opportunity evidence insufficient.

Show the deterministic result change.

## Step 7 — District view

Show the 500 synthetic beneficiary dataset.

Calculate:
- observed interest
- capacity
- potential mismatch.

## Step 8 — Evidence Brief

Generate:
- district
- period
- observed interest
- capacity
- potential mismatch
- evidence gaps
- validation required.

Label:

> AI-assisted analytical brief — human review required.

## Demo rule

Never present synthetic figures as real district statistics.
