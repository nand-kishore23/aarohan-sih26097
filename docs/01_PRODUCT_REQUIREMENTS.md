# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

## PR-01 Voice-first beneficiary intake

The beneficiary should be able to answer conversational questions using voice.

Required information:
- education
- family/traditional occupation
- current livelihood
- existing skills
- interests
- mobility constraints
- physical constraints where relevant
- wage/self-employment preference
- local opportunity context where the beneficiary knows it.

## PR-02 Structured profile

The conversation produces a structured profile with provenance per field.

## PR-03 Informal skill discovery

The system extracts practical activities and maps them to normalized skills only when supported by evidence.

## PR-04 Qualification-backed pathways

Candidate pathways must originate from the canonical qualification catalogue.

## PR-05 Skill gaps

Skill gaps must be calculated by comparing normalized beneficiary skills/evidence with canonical qualification competencies.

## PR-06 Explainability

Every candidate must expose:
- supporting evidence
- missing evidence
- skill gaps
- constraints
- qualification source
- why supported
- why not supported where relevant.

## PR-07 0–3 candidate rule

Never force three.

## PR-08 Stress test

Show pathway weaknesses under access, capacity, opportunity-evidence, skill-gap, and missing-evidence conditions.

## PR-09 District aggregation

Aggregate anonymized profiles into observed interest signals.

## PR-10 Capacity mismatch

Compare observed interest against training capacity.

## PR-11 Evidence Brief

Generate a human-reviewable analytical brief.

## PR-12 Provenance

Every material claim must have provenance.

## PR-13 Missing-data honesty

Missing information must remain missing.

## PR-14 Human decision

The system supports, never replaces, human review.

## PR-15 Demo realism

All synthetic data must be visible as synthetic.
